# Review workflow and handoffs

1. Confirm the brief, whether a scoping review fits the objective, team and resources. Inspect existing work and preserve it.
2. Develop the PCC question, operational definitions and eligibility rules. Draft the protocol before formal review work. Resolve all TBD items, record author agreement and decide whether/where to deposit it. Log later amendments with timing and affected records.
3. Develop database-specific strategies, test retrieval of known relevant papers and document search peer review. Select databases and grey-literature sources appropriate to the topic. Save full syntax, platform, filters, execution time, counts and original exports. Record exploratory searches separately from final searches. Plan updates and citation searching.
4. Import each result with its search ID. Preserve source IDs. Deduplicate with a retained-record mapping, method and human resolution of ambiguous matches. Link reports describing the same underlying study without treating different reports as duplicates automatically.
5. Pilot eligibility decisions, resolve ambiguities and version the rules. Plan two independent human reviewers for both title/abstract and full-text selection, with consensus or third-reviewer adjudication. Keep AI suggestions separate from human decisions. Record one principal criterion-based full-text exclusion reason; retrieval failure is a separate state.
6. Pilot the extraction form on varied sources. Record form changes and recheck earlier extractions. Extract evidence with page/table/section locators, units and missing-value codes; record human verification and disagreements. Decide explicitly whether critical appraisal serves this review's purpose.
7. Map evidence against the questions. Define denominators and report/study units; document coding and any calculations. Avoid causal or effectiveness conclusions unsupported by the designs and review purpose.
8. Reconcile the selection flow from ledgers, prepare tables, draft the manuscript, audit every claim/citation and complete PRISMA-ScR locations. Report limitations, amendments, funding, contributions and AI assistance.
9. Refresh searches if the team requires it; archive the final protocol, strategies, permissible data and analysis code. Review journal requirements and sharing rights before release.

Each work session: update task status, decision log, amendments if relevant, and AI activity. Human sign-offs must be made by the named person; never fabricate them.
