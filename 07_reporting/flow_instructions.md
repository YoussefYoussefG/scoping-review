# Selection-flow accounting

Enter counts only when reconciled against the relevant ledgers. Blank means not calculated. Separate database/register records from other-source identification as appropriate. Define record, report and study units. Record duplicates and other pre-screen removals separately; do not use deduplication to collapse distinct reports from one study.

Check: identified database/register records minus pre-screen removals = records screened; screened minus title/abstract exclusions = reports sought only after record-to-report reconciliation; reports sought minus not retrieved = reports assessed; assessed minus full-text exclusions = included reports. Document other-source branches and overlap before combining totals. Included studies come from unique study IDs, not the report count. No flow diagram should show fabricated zero counts while work is pending.
